## 11. Full Article

# How to Cancel Your Scribd Subscription

Paying for Scribd but barely using it? Here's the one rule that matters: **cancel where you subscribed.** Signed up on the website? Cancel on the website. Signed up through the iPhone app? Cancel in the App Store. Through Android? Cancel in Google Play. Canceling in the wrong place is the number-one reason people keep getting charged after "canceling."

**Key takeaways**

- Cancel through the same channel you subscribed through — website, App Store, or Google Play.
- You keep access until the end of your current billing period; canceling doesn't cut you off instantly.
- Confirm it worked: look for the confirmation email and check your next statement.
- Still being charged after canceling? You almost certainly subscribed through an app store.
- Steps change over time — if anything below looks different, check Scribd's help pages for the current flow.

## Cancel on the Web

If you subscribed directly on Scribd's website:

1. Log in at scribd.com.
2. Open your account settings and find the membership or subscription section.
3. Choose the option to cancel or end your membership, and confirm.

Scribd will usually offer you a pause or a discount first — you can decline and confirm the cancellation. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

Exact labels and layout change over time, so if the page looks different from what you expected, use Scribd's help pages for the current steps rather than guessing.

## Cancel on iPhone

If you subscribed through the iPhone app, canceling on Scribd's website **won't stop the charges** — Apple bills you, so Apple is where you cancel:

1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
3. Find Scribd in the list and cancel it there.

Do this at least a day before your renewal date to be safe. Apple confirms subscription changes on screen — make sure you see that confirmation.

## Cancel on Android

Same logic on Android — if Google Play bills you, Google Play is where you cancel:

1. Open the Play Store app.
2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
3. Find Scribd and cancel it.

Again: give it at least a day's buffer before renewal, and confirm the cancellation went through on screen.

## Check You're Actually Canceled

Don't trust your memory on this one — verify:

- [ ] You saw an on-screen confirmation of the cancellation
- [ ] You received a confirmation email (check spam too)
- [ ] Your account/subscription page shows the membership ending, not renewing
- [ ] Your next card or store statement shows no new Scribd charge

If any box is unchecked, go back and redo the step — a half-finished cancellation is the same as no cancellation.

## Still Being Charged? Diagnose It

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Canceled on the website, charges continue | You subscribed through Apple or Google | Cancel in the App Store / Play Store (sections above) |
| Canceled in the store, charges continue | Cancellation didn't complete, or there's a second account | Check the store's subscription list again; check for a second Scribd account with another email |
| Charged right after the trial ended | The trial auto-converted (that's the standard model) | Cancel now, then ask support about the recent charge |
| Charges under a name you don't recognize | Family member's subscription, or an old account | Ask around the household; search your email for Scribd receipts |

Work down the table in order — the first row covers the large majority of cases.

## What About a Refund?

Sometimes. Scribd has been known to refund recent charges when people contact support promptly after an unwanted renewal — but it's decided case by case, and there's no guaranteed refund policy to quote here. Your best move: cancel first (so it doesn't happen again), then contact Scribd support about the specific charge, politely and promptly. The sooner after the charge, the better your odds.

Don't buy "refund services" from third-party sites that promise to get your money back for a fee. That's a separate scam feeding on this exact situation.

## A Free Alternative for Occasional Documents

For publicly visible Scribd documents, a free downloader gives you the PDF with no account, no card, and nothing to cancel later. The box at the top of the homepage handles it.

## FAQ

**How do I cancel my Scribd subscription?**
On the web through your account settings, or in the App Store / Google Play subscriptions if you signed up through an app. The rule: cancel where you subscribed.

**Will I lose access immediately after canceling?**
No — you keep access until the end of your current billing period. Canceling stops the next charge, not today's access.

**I canceled on the website but I'm still being charged — why?**
You almost certainly subscribed through Apple or Google. Store subscriptions must be canceled in the store, not on Scribd's website.

**Can I get a refund from Scribd?**
Sometimes, for recent charges, if you contact support promptly — it's decided case by case. Cancel first, then ask.

**How do I know the cancellation worked?**
On-screen confirmation, a confirmation email, and no charge on your next statement. Check all three.

**Is there a free way to get Scribd documents?**
For publicly visible documents, yes — paste the link into a free downloader and get the PDF with no account or payment.

## The Bottom Line

Cancel where you subscribed, confirm it in three places (screen, email, statement), and don't assume — verify. And if the whole reason you subscribed was a document or two, the free tool on the homepage covers that need without a subscription to cancel next time.
